TECHNIQUE:
This is a lumbar spine MRI performed on patient 0112. The study was performed on a 1.5 Tesla scanner with the following parameters: T1-weighted sagittal and axial images, T2-weighted sagittal and axial images, and T2-weighted fat-saturated sagittal and axial images.

FINDINGS:
The lumbar spine MRI demonstrates Pfirrmann grade 3 disc degeneration at L3-L4, L4-L5, and L5-S1. At L3-L4, there is a disc bulge with thecal sac compression. At L4-L5, there is a disc protrusion with disc bulge and thecal sac compression. At L5-S1, there is a disc protrusion with disc bulge, thecal sac compression, and nerve root compression.

IMPRESSION:
The lumbar spine MRI demonstrates Pfirrmann grade 3 disc degeneration at L3-L4, L4-L5, and L5-S1. There is a disc bulge with thecal sac compression at L3-L4, a disc protrusion with disc bulge and thecal sac compression at L4-L5, and a disc protrusion with disc bulge, thecal sac compression, and nerve root compression at L5-S1. These findings are consistent with degenerative disc disease.
