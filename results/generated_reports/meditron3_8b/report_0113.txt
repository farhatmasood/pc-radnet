TECHNIQUE:
This is a lumbar spine MRI performed on a 1.5 Tesla scanner. The patient was positioned supine with the head in neutral position. The sequence used was T2-weighted, sagittal and axial, and T1-weighted, sagittal and axial. The field of view was 24 cm, the matrix was 256x256, and the slice thickness was 3 mm.

FINDINGS:
The lumbar spine shows Pfirrmann grade 2 discs at L3-L4, L4-L5, and L5-S1. At L3-L4, there is a disc bulge with thecal sac compression and nerve root compression. At L4-L5, there is a disc protrusion with disc bulge, thecal sac compression, and nerve root compression. At L5-S1, there is a disc protrusion with disc bulge and thecal sac compression.

IMPRESSION:
The lumbar spine MRI shows Pfirrmann grade 2 discs at L3-L4, L4-L5, and L5-S1. There is a disc bulge with thecal sac compression and nerve root compression at L3-L4. There is a disc protrusion with disc bulge, thecal sac compression, and nerve root compression at L4-L5. There is a disc protrusion with disc bulge and thecal sac compression at L5-S1. These findings are consistent with degenerative disc disease.
