TECHNIQUE:
This is a lumbar spine MRI performed on patient 0062. The study was done on a 1.5 Tesla scanner with a 3D T1-weighted sequence, a 3D T2-weighted sequence, and a 2D T2-weighted sequence. The patient was positioned supine with the head in neutral position. The study was performed without contrast.

FINDINGS:
The lumbar spine MRI shows Pfirrmann grade 2 discs at L3-L4, L4-L5, and L5-S1. At L3-L4, there is a disc bulge with thecal sac compression and nerve root compression. At L4-L5, there is a disc bulge with nerve root compression. At L5-S1, there is a disc bulge.

IMPRESSION:
The lumbar spine MRI shows Pfirrmann grade 2 discs at L3-L4, L4-L5, and L5-S1. There is a disc bulge with thecal sac compression and nerve root compression at L3-L4. There is a disc bulge with nerve root compression at L4-L5. There is a disc bulge at L5-S1. These findings are consistent with degenerative disc disease.
