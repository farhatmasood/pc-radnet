**Lumbar Spine MRI Report**

**Technique:** Standardized axial T2-weighted imaging with diffusion-weighted sequences was performed using a 3T MRI scanner.

**Findings:**
- **L3-L4**: The intervertebral disc at L3-L4 shows moderate disc bulge with slight narrowing of the central annulus fibrosus, consistent with Pfirrmann Grade 2 abnormality. There is mild thecal sac compression noted due to this bulge, along with mild nerve root compression affecting the right lateral aspect of the L4 nerve root.
- **L4-L5**: The intervertebral disc at L4-L5 exhibits significant disc bulge with marked narrowing of the central annulus fibrosus, corresponding to Pfirrmann Grade 2 abnormality. This bulge also causes severe thecal sac compression and moderate nerve root compression affecting both the left and right lateral aspects of the L5 nerve roots.
