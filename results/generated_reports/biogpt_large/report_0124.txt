- L5-S1: Pfirrmann grade 3; disc height 10.8 pixels; disc protrusion, disc bulge, thecal sac compression, nerve root compression.

IMPRESSION:
- L3 / 4--no abnormality seen on review of images and patient history. - L4.-5.
