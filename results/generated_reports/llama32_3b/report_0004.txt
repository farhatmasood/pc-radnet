The MRI examination was performed using a 1.5T MRI scanner with a 32-channel coil. The images were obtained using a T2-weighted sequence with a 3mm slice thickness and no contrast enhancement.

* L3-L4:
  - The intervertebral disc at L3-L4 is of Pfirrmann grade 2, with a disc height of 9.56 mm.

The lumbar spine MRI examination reveals multiple disc bulges and thecal sac compression at the L3-L4, L4-L5, and L5-S1 levels.
