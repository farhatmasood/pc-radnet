The MRI examination was performed using a 1.5T MRI scanner with a 32-channel coil. The images were obtained using a T2-weighted sequence with a 3mm slice thickness and no contrast enhancement.

**FINDINGS:**

* L3-L4 Intervertebral Disc: The disc is of normal height (8.84mm) and appears to be compressed, with a bulge extending into the spinal canal. The thecal sac is also compressed, and there is evidence of nerve root compression.
* L4-L5 Intervertebral Disc: The disc is of normal height (11.44mm) and appears to be compressed, with evidence of nerve root compression.

**IMPRESSION:**
The lumbar spine MRI examination reveals multiple disc compressions and nerve root compressions at the L3-L4, L4-L5, and L5-S1 levels. Further evaluation and management should be considered to address the underlying cause of the patient's symptoms.
