MRI of the lumbar spine was performed using T1- and T2-weighted sequences in both axial and sagittal planes.

FINDINGS:
At L3-L4, the intervertebral disc demonstrates mild disc degeneration with preserved disc height (Pfirrmann grade 2). disc height is preserved. there is disc protrusion, disc bulge, and thecal sac compression. At L4-L5, the intervertebral disc demonstrates moderate disc degeneration with decreased signal intensity (Pfirrmann grade 3). disc height is preserved. there is disc protrusion, disc bulge, and thecal sac compression. At L5-S1, the intervertebral disc demonstrates advanced disc degeneration with significant loss of disc height (Pfirrmann grade 4). disc height is mildly reduced. there is disc protrusion, disc bulge, and thecal sac compression.

IMPRESSION:
1. L3-L4: disc protrusion, disc bulge, thecal sac compression.
2. L4-L5: moderate disc degeneration, disc protrusion, disc bulge, thecal sac compression.
3. L5-S1: advanced disc degeneration, disc protrusion, disc bulge, thecal sac compression.
