**3. IMPRESSION:**

There is evidence of degenerative disc disease at L3-L4, L4-L5, and L5-S1. The Pfirrmann Grade II signal intensity suggests the presence of degenerative changes, likely including disc herniation. Disc bulges, disc protrusions, and thecal sac compression are present at multiple levels. Nerve root compression is also noted at multiple levels. The degree of compression may warrant further clinical correlation.
