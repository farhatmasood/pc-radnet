## Lumbar Spine MRI

**1. TECHNIQUE:**

MRI of the lumbar spine was performed with [Specify Sequence, e.g., T1-weighted with sagittal and axial reformats]. Sagittal and axial images were obtained.

**2. FINDINGS:**

**L3-L4:**

*   **Disc:** Pfirrmann Grade III (inhomogeneous, intermediate gray signal, normal to slightly decreased disc height). Disc height measured 8.9 pixels.
*   **Findings:** Disc protrusion, disc bulge, and thecal sac compression are present.

**L4-L5:**

*   **Disc:** Pfirrmann Grade IV (inhomogeneous, dark gray to black signal, slightly decreased disc height). Disc height measured 9.0 pixels.
*   **Findings:** Disc protrusion, disc bulge, thecal sac compression, and nerve root compression are present.

**L5-S1:**

*   **Disc:** Pfirrmann Grade IV (inhomogeneous, dark gray to black signal, slightly decreased disc height). Disc height measured 5.6 pixels.
*   **Findings:** Disc bulge and thecal sac compression are present.

**3. IMPRESSION:**

There is evidence of degenerative disc disease at L3-L4, L4-L5, and L5-S1. The L4-L5 and L5-S1 discs demonstrate Pfirrmann Grade IV signal changes, indicating significant disc degeneration. These findings are associated with disc protrusion, disc bulge, thecal sac compression, and nerve root compression at L4-L5 and L5-S1. The L3-L4 disc shows less severe degenerative changes.
