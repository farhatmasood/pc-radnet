## Lumbar Spine MRI

**1. TECHNIQUE:**

MRI of the lumbar spine was performed with [Specify Sequence, e.g., T1-weighted with sagittal and axial reformats]. Sagittal and axial images were obtained.

**2. FINDINGS:**

**L3-L4:**

*   **Disc:** The L3-L4 intervertebral disc shows a Pfirrmann Grade 2/5 lesion. The lesion is inhomogeneous with or without horizontal bands and exhibits bright white signal intensity on T1-weighted images. The disc height is 10.4 pixels.
*   **Disc Protrusion/Bulge:** There is evidence of disc protrusion and disc bulge at the L3-L4 level.
*   **Thecal Sac:** Thecal sac compression is present at the L3-L4 level.

**L4-L5:**

*   **Disc:** The L4-L5 intervertebral disc shows a Pfirrmann Grade 3/5 lesion. The lesion is inhomogeneous with intermediate gray signal intensity on T1-weighted images. The disc height is 10.7 pixels.
*   **Disc Protrusion/Bulge:** There is evidence of disc protrusion and disc bulge at the L4-L5 level.
*   **Thecal Sac:** Thecal sac compression is present at the L4-L5 level.

**L5-S1:**

*   **Disc:** The L5-S1 intervertebral disc shows a Pfirrmann Grade 3/5 lesion. The lesion is inhomogeneous with intermediate gray signal intensity on T1-weighted images. The disc height is 10.9 pixels.
*   **Disc Protrusion/Bulge:** There is evidence of disc protrusion and disc bulge at the L5-S1 level.
*   **Thecal Sac:** Thecal sac compression is present at the L5-S1 level.

**3. IMPRESSION:**

The lumbar spine MRI demonstrates evidence of degenerative disc disease at multiple levels (L3-L4, L4-L5, and L5-S1). Specifically, there are Pfirrmann Grade 2/5 and Grade 3/5 lesions in the L3-L4, L4-L5, and L5-S1 discs, respectively. These lesions are associated with disc protrusion and bulge, and thecal sac compression at each level.
