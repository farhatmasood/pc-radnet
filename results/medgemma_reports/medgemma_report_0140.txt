**3. IMPRESSION:**

There is evidence of degenerative disc disease at multiple levels of the lumbar spine. Specifically, there is a Grade II disc herniation at L3-L4, L4-L5, and L5-S1. These findings are associated with thecal sac compression and nerve root compression at multiple levels.
