TECHNIQUE:
This is a lumbar spine MRI performed on a 0092 patient. The technique used was a T1-weighted sagittal and axial images, T2-weighted sagittal and axial images, and a T2-weighted fat-saturated sagittal and axial images.

FINDINGS:
The lumbar spine MRI shows the following findings:
- L3-L4: Pfirrmann grade 2, disc height 9.47 pixels, disc protrusion, disc bulge, thecal sac compression, and nerve root compression.
- L4-L5: Pfirrmann grade 2, disc height 10.12 pixels, disc protrusion, disc bulge, thecal sac compression, and nerve root compression.
- L5-S1: Pfirrmann grade 4, disc height 7.11 pixels, disc protrusion, disc bulge, thecal sac compression.

IMPRESSION:
The lumbar spine MRI shows Pfirrmann grade 2 at L3-L4 and L4-L5, Pfirrmann grade 4 at L5-S1, disc protrusion, disc bulge, thecal sac compression, and nerve root compression at all levels. These findings are consistent with degenerative disc disease and nerve root compression.
