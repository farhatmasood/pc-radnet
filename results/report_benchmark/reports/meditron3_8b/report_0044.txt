TECHNIQUE:
This is a lumbar spine MRI performed on patient 0044. The study was performed on a 1.5 Tesla scanner with the following parameters: T1-weighted sagittal and axial images, T2-weighted sagittal and axial images, and T2-weighted fat-saturated sagittal and axial images.

At L4-L5, there is a disc bulge with thecal sac compression. At L5-S1, there is a disc bulge with thecal sac compression.
