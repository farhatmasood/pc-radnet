TECHNIQUE:
This is a lumbar spine MRI performed on a 1.5 Tesla scanner. The patient was positioned supine with the head in neutral position. The sequence used was T2-weighted, sagittal and axial, and T1-weighted, sagittal and axial. The field of view was 24 cm, the matrix was 256x256, and the slice thickness was 3 mm.

FINDINGS:
The lumbar spine shows degenerative changes at multiple levels. At L3-L4, the Pfirrmann grade is 2, indicating normal disc height. However, there is a disc bulge with thecal sac compression. At L4-L5, the Pfirrmann grade is 3, indicating mild disc height loss. There is a disc protrusion, disc bulge, and thecal sac compression. At L5-S1, the Pfirrmann grade is 3, indicating mild disc height loss. There is a disc protrusion, disc bulge, and thecal sac compression.

IMPRESSION:
The lumbar spine MRI shows degenerative changes at multiple levels. There is a disc bulge with thecal sac compression at L3-L4, disc protrusion, disc bulge, and thecal sac compression at L4-L5, and disc protrusion, disc bulge, and thecal sac compression at L5-S1. These findings are consistent with degenerative disc disease.
