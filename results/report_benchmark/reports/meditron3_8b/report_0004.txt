TECHNIQUE:
This is a lumbar spine MRI performed on patient 0004. The technique used was a T1-weighted sagittal and axial images, T2-weighted sagittal and axial images, and a T2-weighted fat-saturated sagittal image.

FINDINGS:
The lumbar spine MRI shows Pfirrmann grade 2 discs at L3-L4, L4-L5, and L5-S1. At L3-L4, there is a disc bulge with thecal sac compression and nerve root compression. At L4-L5, there is a disc bulge with nerve root compression. At L5-S1, there is a disc bulge with thecal sac compression.

IMPRESSION:
The lumbar spine MRI shows Pfirrmann grade 2 discs at L3-L4, L4-L5, and L5-S1. There is a disc bulge with thecal sac compression and nerve root compression at L3-L4, a disc bulge with nerve root compression at L4-L5, and a disc bulge with thecal sac compression at L5-S1. These findings are consistent with degenerative disc disease.
