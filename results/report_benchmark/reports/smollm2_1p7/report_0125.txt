Lumbar Spine MRI Report

TECHNIQUE:
The MRI was performed using a 1.5 Tesla Magnet and a 1.5 Tesla Magnet with a 3 Tesla Magnet. The imaging sequence used was a T1-weighted post-injection spoiled gradient echo (GRE) with a repetition time (TR) of 2000 msec and a flip angle of 71 degrees. The imaging protocol included a sagittal T1-weighted image, a coronal T1-weighted image, and a sagittal T2-weighted image.

FINDINGS:
The lumbar spine MRI revealed the following abnormalities:

L3-L4:
- Disc herniation (grade 2) with a disc height of 8.69 pixels.
- Thecal sac compression (grade 2).
- Nerve root compression (grade 2).

L4-L5:
- Disc herniation (grade 2) with a disc height of 9.91 pixels.
