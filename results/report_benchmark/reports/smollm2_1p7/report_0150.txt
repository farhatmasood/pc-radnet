Lumbar Spine MRI Report

TECHNIQUE:
The MRI was performed using a 1.5 Tesla Magnet and a 1.5 Tesla Magnet with a 3 Tesla Magnet. The imaging sequence was a T1-weighted post-injection spoiled gradient echo (GRE) with a repetition time (TR) of 2000 msec and a flip angle of 71 degrees. The imaging parameters were a slice thickness of 1.5 mm, a field of view of 15 cm, and a matrix of 256 x 256.

FINDINGS:
The lumbar spine MRI revealed the following abnormalities:

L3-L4:
- Disc protrusion (grade 2) with a disc height of 11.06 pixels.
- Disc bulge (grade 2) with a disc height of 10.91 pixels.
- Thecal sac compression (grade 3) with a disc height of 7.16 pixels.

L4
