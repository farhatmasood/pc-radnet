Lumbar Spine MRI Report

TECHNIQUE:
The MRI was performed using a 1.5 Tesla Magnet and a 1.5 Tesla Magnet with a 3 Tesla Magnet. The imaging sequence used was a T1-weighted post-injection spoiled gradient echo (GRE) with a repetition time (TR) of 2000 msec and a flip angle of 71 degrees. The imaging protocol included a sagittal and a coronal plane acquisition.
