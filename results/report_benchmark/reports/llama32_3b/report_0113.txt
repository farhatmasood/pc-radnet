**Lumbar Spine MRI Report**

**Patient ID:** 0113

**TECHNIQUE:**
The MRI examination was performed using a 1.5 Tesla MRI scanner with a 3T spinal coil. The images were obtained using a T2-weighted sequence with a 3mm slice thickness and no contrast enhancement.

**FINDINGS:**

* L3-L4:
  - The intervertebral disc at L3-L4 is of Pfirrmann grade 2, with a disc height of 8.69 mm.
  - A disc bulge is present at L3-L4, causing thecal sac compression and nerve root compression.
* L4-L5:
  - The intervertebral disc at L4-L5 is of Pfirrmann grade 2, with a disc height of 9.34 mm.
  - A disc protrusion is present at L4-L5, causing thecal sac compression and nerve root compression.
* L5-S1:
  - The intervertebral disc at L5-S1 is of Pfirrmann grade 2, with a disc height of 9.09 mm.

**IMPRESSION:**
The lumbar spine MRI examination reveals multiple disc abnormalities, including disc bulges and protrusions at L3-L4, L4-L5, and L5-S1. These abnormalities are associated with thecal sac compression and nerve root compression. These findings are consistent with a lumbar disc herniation, which may be contributing to the patient's symptoms. Further evaluation and consultation with a neurologist or orthopedic surgeon
