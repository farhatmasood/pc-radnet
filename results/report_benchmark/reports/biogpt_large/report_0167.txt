TECHNIQUE: Lumbar spine MRI was reviewed using the provided structured measurements.

FINDINGS:
- L3-L4: Pfirrmann grade 2; disc height 8.8 pixels; disc bulge, thecal sac compression, nerve root compression.
- L4-L5: Pfirrmann grade 2; disc height 11.4 pixels; nerve root compression.
- L5-S1: Pfirrmann grade 2; disc height 11.5 pixels; disc bulge, thecal sac compression, nerve root compression.

IMPRESSION:
- L1 / L2: Pfirr 1 + 2; disc space < 2mm; facet joint hypertrophy; spondylolisthesis > 5%. - L2 / 3: Pfirri 1 + 2, disc space < 3mm; facet arthrosis; listhesis > 5mm. - L3 / 4: Pfirre 1 + 2 and disc space < 5mm.The patient had no symptoms at follow up. CONCLUSIONS: This case illustrates that it is possible to use structural measures of degenerative changes on routine clinical imaging studies in order to make an accurate diagnosis of symptomatic spinal stenosis.
